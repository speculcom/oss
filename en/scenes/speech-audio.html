How to choose

• Fast local transcription: Whisper variants; streaming: check chunking and VAD.
• Speaker labels and timestamps: test on your own recordings.
• Voice cloning needs explicit consent and an abuse-review path.
Not for: Do not upload confidential audio to a service you have not reviewed.
