RAG & Knowledge Bases

Retrieval quality depends on chunking, recall, permissions and evaluation; choose a stack whose sources you can inspect.

See all in this scene → Compare first 3 Home
20selected repos
For teams grounding answers in private documents and needing citations.Who it is for

How to choose

• Mixed formats and permissions: check parsing, chunking and ACL support first.
• Need a ready UI and citations: RAGFlow; code-first pipelines: LlamaIndex.
• Measure retrieval separately from generation; keep the source path.
Not for: Do not use RAG to patch missing source data or unclear permissions.
